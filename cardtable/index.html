How to play

  • Drag cards and piles to move them around the table.
  • Click a card or pile for actions: flip, rotate, shuffle, deal, draw, delete.
  • Double-click a card to flip it; double-click a pile to draw into your hand.
  • Drop a card onto a pile to place it on top; onto another card to start a pile; drop a pile onto a pile to merge them.
  • Drag a card to the bottom bar to take it into your hand (hands are private). Drag the bar's top edge to resize your hand cards.
  • Click a card in your hand — or 🔍 on a face-up table card — to see it enlarged.
  • Peek shows you a face-down card (or the top of a pile) privately — but everyone sees in the log that you peeked.
  • Everyone gets a seat around the table, and the table auto-turns so your seat is at the bottom. Cards you play land facing you.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty felt to pan; on touch screens, pinch with two fingers to zoom, rotate and pan at once. Your view is yours alone — 🧭 resets it to your seat.
  • The host can enlarge the table (Table: small / medium / large) when more players join.
  • 📜 Rules: the host can write a script that enforces rules (turn order, legal moves) and automates play (auto-dealing, a poker dealer). Everyone can read the active script; everything it does appears in the log as "📜 Rules".
  • Drag from your hand to the table to play it — toggle 🂠 face-down to play cards face down.
  • Copy invite and send the link to friends — or tap QR so they can scan it with a phone. Everyone sees the table live over WebRTC (no server).
  • The game lives in the host's browser tab — if the host closes it, reopening the same link on the host's browser resumes the game.
