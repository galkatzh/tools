Jam

Play music together over the internet, NINJAM style.

Time is split into intervals. While you play, you hear what everyone else played in the previous interval, and they hear you one interval later, so lag never knocks anyone off the beat. Only notes travel; every player brings their own synth.

Everyone who opens the same room joins the same jam. Players connect peer-to-peer over WebRTC.
