Ultrasonic test

Can two phones hear each other above 15 kHz from a web page? Open this page on both, turn the media volume up, tap Send on one and Listen on the other, and hold both still. Send and Listen together on one phone test its own speaker against its own mic.

Plays 15 to 21 kHz in 0.5 kHz steps, 0.35 s each, over and over.

Frequency in kHz, newest at the top; the blue bar marks the test tones. A dark stretch on the right means the mic path cuts those frequencies off.

    How far each tone rose above the noise around it, at its best over the last round: 25 dB or more is clear, 15 to 25 faint, under 15 not heard (noise alone reaches about 12).
